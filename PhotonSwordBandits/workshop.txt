title=Photon Sword - Bandits
description=[h1]Photon Sword - Bandits[/h1]
description=A compatibility patch that brings Photon Sword to Bandits.
description=
description=[h2]NPCs with photon swords[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Bandits who carry a melee weapon can carry an ignited photon sword instead, at the [b]Photon Sword Chance[/b] (default 2%). Each NPC rolls once and keeps its sword. NPCs set up with a photon sword in the creator always carry one.
description=[*]They carry it switched off, ignite it as a fight starts or as they close in, and put it out once the fight is over.
description=[*]Lit, their blades glow, cast light, and hum like yours, in a random color per NPC or one you pick.
description=[*]They fight with it, using Photon Sword's damage and Blade Damage Multiplier.
description=[*]Kill one and the sword drops. Pick it up and it comes with a part-drained battery fitted.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Deflecting their gunfire[/h2]
description=A photon sword's raised guard (the ready stance, or a Form's block with Photon Sword Forms) deflects bandits' bullets and soaks their shotgun blasts by the same rules and sandbox options as other gunfire, and a deflected bullet strikes the bandit back. With Photon Sword Forms, deflections drain the guard meter.
description=
description=[h2]Blocking their swords[/h2]
description=The same guard blocks a bandit's photon sword strike from in front, by Photon Sword's Block Chance options (always, with a Form's block, while the meter lasts), and a bandit carrying one blocks yours now and then when it faces you (Photon Sword's NPC Block Chance, default 35%). NPCs with swords block each other's too (NPC vs NPC Block Chance, default 20%). A blocking NPC parries, with the same animation as your ready stance's.
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options (Photon Sword page, Bandits NPCs section)[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Photon Sword Chance (%):[/b] default 2; 0 means no NPC carries one.
description=[*][b]NPC Blade Color:[/b] Random (default), or one of the eight blade colors.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Running both NPC patches[/h2]
description=You'll usually want one NPC mod at a time. If you do run Bandits and Project A-Life together, a bandit's sword blocks an A-Life NPC's strike but not the other way round: A-Life hits bandits as ordinary zombies, out of reach of the patch.
description=
description=Requires Photon Sword and Bandits. Works with Photon Sword Forms too. Build 42.22 or later.
