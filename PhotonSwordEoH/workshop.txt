title=Photon Sword - Echoes of Humanity
description=[h1]Photon Sword - Echoes of Humanity[/h1]
description=A compatibility patch that brings Photon Sword to Echoes of Humanity.
description=
description=[h2]NPCs with photon swords[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Echoes of Humanity NPCs who carry a melee weapon can carry an ignited photon sword instead, at the [b]Photon Sword Chance[/b] (default 2%). Each NPC rolls once and keeps its sword.
description=[*]They carry it switched off, ignite it as a fight starts, and put it out once the fight is over.
description=[*]Lit, their blades glow, cast light, and hum like yours, in a random color per NPC or one you pick.
description=[*]They fight with it, using Photon Sword's damage and Blade Damage Multiplier.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Deflecting their gunfire[/h2]
description=A photon sword's raised guard (the ready stance, or a Form's block with Photon Sword Forms) deflects Echoes of Humanity NPCs' bullets back at them and soaks their shotgun blasts, by the same rules and sandbox options as other gunfire.
description=
description=[h2]Blocking their swords[/h2]
description=The same guard blocks an NPC's photon sword strike from in front, by Photon Sword's Block Chance options, and an NPC carrying one blocks yours now and then when it faces you (NPC Block Chance, default 35%). NPCs with swords block each other's too (NPC vs NPC Block Chance, default 20%).
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options (Photon Sword page, Echoes of Humanity NPCs section)[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Photon Sword Chance (%):[/b] default 2; 0 means no NPC carries one.
description=[*][b]NPC Blade Color:[/b] Random (default), or one of the eight blade colors.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=Echoes of Humanity runs its NPCs' fights in Java, so this patch carries a small Java plugin for Faded's Java Loader: it hands every hit involving an Echoes of Humanity NPC to Photon Sword's Lua before it lands. Enabling or updating it needs a full game restart, as with any Java Loader mod.
description=
description=Requires Photon Sword, Echoes of Humanity, and Faded's Java Loader with its Bridge. Works with Photon Sword Forms too. Build 42.22.
description=
