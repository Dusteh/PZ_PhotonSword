     * Two items that are swapped by Lua (see shared/PhotonSword/PS_Core.lua):
     *   PhotonSwordHilt  inert hilt: light, nearly harmless, one-handed
     *   PhotonSword      ignited blade: modeled on Base.Katana. Its color comes from the igniting
     *                   player's Blade Color option; Blue below is only the default model. It shares
     *                   the hilt's icon, so its inventory and hotbar icon don't change on ignite.
     * Both share AttachmentType = Knife so a hotbar binding survives the swap.
     * Blade swing/hit sounds come from sounds_photonsword.txt; both are re-pointed per player's
     * tone/pitch options at runtime (PS_Hum.lua). Models, textures, and the icon come from tools/gen_models.py;
     * a blade's model is switched to its crystal's color at ignition (PS.swap).
     *
     * Photon crystals, one per palette color (PS.BLADE_COLORS), are weapon parts in their own
     * PartType = Crystal slot, mounted on the hilt the way vanilla mounts scopes on guns: the game's
     * Add / Remove Weapon Upgrade menu fits and removes them, and PS.swap carries the crystal
     * between hilt and blade. A hilt ignites only with one fitted (ISPhotonSwordToggle), and only
     * an inert hilt gives one up (CanDetach). No stat modifiers, so fitting one changes nothing else.
     */
