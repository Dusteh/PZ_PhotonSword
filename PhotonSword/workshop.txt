title=Photon Sword
description=[h1]Photon Sword[/h1]
description=A battery-powered, two-handed energy blade for Project Zomboid Build 42. Find a hilt, power it with a battery, and ignite it. A lit blade hums, glows in your chosen color, never gets bloody, and (if the server allows it) cuts through doors, walls, objects, and trees.
description=
description=[h2]Getting started[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Find a hilt.[/b] Photon Sword Hilts are rare loot in sword displays, late-game melee weapon stashes, and junk crates. They spawn without a battery.
description=[*][b]Power it.[/b] Right-click the hilt and choose [b]Insert Battery[/b]. Any charged battery works; its charge moves into the hilt. [b]Swap Battery[/b] and [b]Remove Battery[/b] work on an unlit hilt.
description=[*][b]Ignite it.[/b] Right-click and choose [b]Ignite[/b], or press [b]Z[/b]. With empty hands, Z draws your sword (hotbar first) and ignites it. Press Z again to extinguish. Z also works from the aiming stance.
description=[*][b]Watch the power.[/b] A lit blade drains its battery (8 in-game hours per full battery by default), warns you when power runs low, and switches itself off when empty. Every lit blade you carry drains, even in a bag.
description=[*][b]Put it away.[/b] Putting a lit blade back on your hotbar extinguishes it automatically (can be turned off).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Stance[/h2]
description=An ignited sword is held in its own stance instead of a bat's. Out of combat you carry it low in the sword hand, the off hand free, standing, sneaking, and walking. Aim and you drop into a two-handed ready stance, blade up toward your target; stand in it a while and your off hand may beckon them on. Aiming an unlit hilt can beckon too. Prefer the game's own two-handed animations? Tick [b]Default Two-Handed Animations[/b] in the mod options.
description=
description=[h2]Deflecting gunfire[/h2]
description=Aiming in the ready stance raises your guard. A bullet fired from in front of you has a chance to be deflected straight back into the shooter: 10% with no Long Blade skill, up to 80% at Long Blade 10. Shotgun blasts are too wide to send back, so your guard soaks part of the blast instead (10% to 50% by skill), and at Long Blade 10 it can take the whole blast. Works against other players' guns; patch mods add Project A-Life and Bandits NPCs.
description=
description=[h2]Running[/h2]
description=Start running or sprinting with a lit blade and it switches off, then relights in the same color when you stop (unless you lit it again yourself, put it away, or the battery is empty). Servers can turn this off.
description=
description=[h2]Hotkeys[/h2]
description=All rebindable under Options > Mods > Photon Sword.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Z: Ignite / Extinguish.[/b] Ignored in vehicles, where Z switches seats.
description=[*][b]R: Swap Battery.[/b] Loads your fullest battery into the held (or first) hilt and returns the old one. R still reloads guns, sharpens blades, and rotates placements when those apply.
description=[*][b]X: Toggle Cutting.[/b] Only while a lit blade is in hand. Cutting starts [b]off[/b] for every player; press X to turn it on for your swings. X still racks guns.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Cutting[/h2]
description=With cutting on, each swing of a lit blade cuts what you're facing, as far as the server's sandbox options allow: one door, window, wall, or fence on the edge you face, one object in the square in front, and trees, bushes, and tall grass. Containers drop their contents, and each cut plays the matching break sound. Floors, water, curbs, and litter are never cut, and neither is anything inside another player's safehouse.
description=
description=[h2]Mod options (per player)[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Hotkeys:[/b] the three keys above, Draw When Hands Are Empty, Extinguish When Holstered.
description=[*][b]Visuals:[/b] Blade Color (Blue, Green, Red, Purple, Yellow, Orange, Cyan, White), Blade Light on/off, Blade Light Radius, Default Two-Handed Animations, Beckon Chance.
description=[*][b]Sound:[/b] Blade Hum Volume, plus a tone (Smooth, Classic, Gritty) and pitch for the hum, the swing, and the spark on hit.
description=[*][b]Deflect:[/b] Show Deflect Text, Deflect Spark Sound.
description=[*][b]Notifications:[/b] Low Power Warning threshold.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options (Photon Sword page)[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Battery Life (In-Game Hours):[/b] default 8; 0 means blades never drain.
description=[*][b]Blade Damage Multiplier:[/b] default 1.0; applies to blades ignited after the change.
description=[*][b]Extinguish While Running:[/b] default on.
description=[*][b]Destroy Doors and Windows:[/b] default on.
description=[*][b]Destroy Non-Player Walls:[/b] default off.
description=[*][b]Destroy World Objects:[/b] default on. Map furniture, appliances, containers, and fences.
description=[*][b]Destroy Player-Built Objects:[/b] default off. Anything players built.
description=[*][b]Instant Chop / Clear Vegetation:[/b] default on.
description=[*][b]Cut Effect:[/b] Destroy (default) removes targets outright; Set on Fire starts a fire instead, on the far side of the edge or in the square in front, never on your own square. Fires spread normally.
description=[*][b]Deflect Bullets:[/b] default on. [b]Deflect Chance at No Skill / Full Skill:[/b] 10% / 80%. [b]Deflected Bullets Hit the Shooter:[/b] default on.
description=[*][b]Soak Shotgun Blasts:[/b] default on. [b]Blast Soaked at No Skill / Full Skill:[/b] 10% / 50%. [b]Whole Blast Blocked at Full Skill:[/b] 60%.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
