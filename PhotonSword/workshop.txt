title=Photon Sword
description=[h1]Photon Sword[/h1]
description=A battery-powered, two-handed energy blade for Project Zomboid Build 42. Find a hilt, fit a photon crystal and a battery, and ignite it. A lit blade hums, glows in its crystal's color, never gets bloody, and (if the server allows it) cuts through doors, walls, objects, and trees.
description=
description=[h2]Getting started[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Find or build a hilt.[/b] Photon Sword Hilts are rare loot in sword displays, late-game melee weapon stashes, and junk crates. They spawn without a battery. With Electricity 4 you can build one instead ([b]Make Photon Sword Hilt[/b], Electrical): any flashlight with its battery removed, a metal pipe, an amplifier, 2 electronics scrap, 2 electric wire, screws, and 2 duct tape, with a screwdriver, pliers, and snips or a metal saw.
description=[*][b]Fit a crystal.[/b] A hilt ignites only with a photon crystal fitted, and the blade takes its color (Blue, Green, Red, Purple, Yellow, Orange, Cyan, White). Some hilts are found with one already in; crystals also turn up in jewelry stores and with the hilts, and now and then among the stones you spot in search mode (Foraging 2+). Fit or swap one through the game's own [b]Add Weapon Upgrade[/b] menu on an unlit hilt, and take it out with [b]Remove Weapon Upgrade[/b], just like a scope on a gun, so attachment UI mods work with them too.
description=[*][b]Power it.[/b] Right-click the hilt and choose [b]Insert Battery[/b]. Any charged battery works; its charge moves into the hilt. [b]Swap Battery[/b] and [b]Remove Battery[/b] work on an unlit hilt.
description=[*][b]Ignite it.[/b] Right-click and choose [b]Ignite[/b], or press [b]Z[/b] with the sword in your hands. Press Z again to extinguish. Z also works from the aiming stance.
description=[*][b]Watch the power.[/b] A lit blade drains its battery (8 in-game hours per full battery by default), warns you when power runs low, and switches itself off when empty. Every lit blade you carry drains, even in a bag.
description=[*][b]Put it away.[/b] Putting a lit blade back on your hotbar extinguishes it automatically (can be turned off).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Stance[/h2]
description=An ignited sword is held in its own stance instead of a bat's. Out of combat you carry it low in the sword hand, the off hand free, standing, sneaking, and walking. Aim and you drop into a two-handed ready stance, blade up toward your target; stand in it a while and your off hand may beckon them on. Aiming an unlit hilt can beckon too. Prefer the game's own two-handed animations? Tick [b]Default Two-Handed Animations[/b] in the mod options.
description=
description=[h2]Deflecting gunfire[/h2]
description=Aiming in the ready stance raises your guard. A bullet from in front may be deflected back into the shooter: 10% with no Long Blade skill, up to 80% at Long Blade 10. Your guard soaks part of a shotgun blast instead (10% to 50%), and at Long Blade 10 can take all of it. Works against players' guns; patch mods add NPCs.
description=
description=[h2]Blocking photon swords[/h2]
description=The same guard stops another photon sword's strike from in front, a player's or an NPC's: 30% with no Long Blade skill, up to 90% at Long Blade 10, with a spark and a parry into a high cross-guard. With Photon Sword Forms, a Form's block holds while its guard meter lasts. NPCs with photon swords (patch mods) block yours, and each other's, now and then.
description=
description=[h2]Running[/h2]
description=Start running or sprinting with a lit blade and it switches off, then relights in the same color when you stop (unless you lit it again yourself, put it away, or the battery is empty). Servers can turn this off.
description=
description=[h2]Hotkeys[/h2]
description=All rebindable under Options > Mods > Photon Sword.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Z: Ignite / Extinguish.[/b] Ignored in vehicles.
description=[*][b]R: Swap Battery.[/b] Loads your fullest battery into the hilt. R still reloads, sharpens, and rotates when those apply.
description=[*][b]X: Toggle Cutting.[/b] Only with a lit blade in hand; starts [b]off[/b]. X still racks guns.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Cutting[/h2]
description=With cutting on, each swing cuts what you face, as far as the server allows: a door, window, wall, or fence, one object in front, and trees, bushes, and grass. Containers drop their contents. Floors, water, and other players' safehouses are never cut.
description=
description=[h2]Mod options (per player)[/h2]
description=The keys above, extinguishing when holstered, blade light and radius, the stance (or the game's two-handed animations) and its beckon, hum volume, a tone and pitch for the hum, swing, and hit, deflect text and sound, and the low-power warning.
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
description=Servers tune everything on the [b]Photon Sword[/b] sandbox page: battery life (8 hours), blade damage, extinguishing while running, how often looted hilts hold a crystal (25%), what cutting may destroy (map doors, windows, objects, and vegetation on; map walls and player-built things off) and whether it burns instead, and every deflect, soak, and block chance above. Each companion mod adds its own section.
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Photon Sword - Project A-Life[/b] and [b]Photon Sword - Bandits[/b]: patch mods that arm those NPCs with photon swords (rarely, by default) and let your guard deflect their gunfire and block their swords.
description=[*][b]Photon Sword Forms[/b]: fighting styles built on Senator's Martial Arms, with a Form's block as the guard. With no Form enabled, a lit sword plays like a katana: no ready-stance deflect, and it stays lit while running.
description=[/list]
description=
