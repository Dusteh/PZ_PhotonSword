title=Photon Sword Forms
description=[h1]Photon Sword Forms[/h1]
description=A companion to Photon Sword and Senator's Martial Arms. Adds photon sword Forms, fighting styles built on Senator's weapon arts: read [b]Photon Sword: Form 1[/b], ignite a photon sword, and fight with Form 1, which trains its own mastery. Choose your Form and its moves in the new [b]Photon Sword[/b] tab of the martial arts window.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Finding Form 1:[/b] the manual [b]Photon Sword: Form 1[/b] turns up with the other martial arts manuals: bookstores, libraries, magazine racks, post offices, and gym and boxing lockers.
description=[*][b]Form 1:[/b] 17 moves unlocked across mastery 0-4, shuffled into combos, and mastery 5 adds a flowing five-cut combo. Each mastery level adds 10% damage (sandbox option).
description=[*][b]Block:[/b] hold Martial Arts Core's Block key while aiming to raise a center guard with the sword, using Core's guard meter, perfect guard, and guard break.
description=[*][b]Deflecting gunfire:[/b] the block deflects bullets and soaks shotgun blasts, by your Form's mastery and Martial Arts level. Each deflection drains the guard meter; empty it and your guard breaks.
description=[*][b]Blocking photon swords:[/b] the block always stops a photon sword's strike from in front while the guard meter lasts, another player's (Martial Arts Core's block) or an NPC's, draining the meter. Each blow the block stops plays a parry: the hit knocks the blade aside and the guard recovers to center.
description=[*][b]Dodges:[/b] press the dodge key while aiming and you leap away with the hilt in your fist, the blade switched off for the leap and relit when you land.
description=[*]Between moves, the sword is held in Photon Sword's own stance.
description=[*][b]No Form:[/b] with no Form learned, or Disable in the Photon Sword tab, a lit photon sword plays like a katana: the game's two-handed animations (or Senator's katana art), no ready-stance deflect, and it stays lit while running.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options (Photon Sword page, Photon Sword Forms section)[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Damage per Form Mastery Level (%):[/b] default 10.
description=[*][b]Extinguish While Dodging:[/b] default on.
description=[*][b]Guard Drain from Gunfire and Blocks:[/b] default 1.0; 0 means deflecting and blocking NPC swords never drain the guard.
description=[/list]
description=Deflect and block chances and the rest are in Photon Sword's sections of the same page.
description=
description=[h2]Mod options (Options > Mods > Photon Sword, Forms: Dodging)[/h2]
description=Photon Sword Dodge Animation (on; off plays Martial Arts Core's Pak Mei dodge) and Relight After Dodge (on).
