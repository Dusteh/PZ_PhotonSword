title=Photon Sword Forms
description=[h1]Photon Sword Forms[/h1]
description=A companion to Photon Sword and Senator's Martial Arms. Adds photon sword Forms, fighting styles built on Senator's weapon arts: read [b]Photon Sword: Form 1[/b], ignite a photon sword, and fight with Form 1, which trains its own mastery. Choose your Form and its moves in the new [b]Photon Sword[/b] tab of the martial arts window.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Form 1:[/b] 17 moves unlocked across mastery 0-4, shuffled into combos. Each mastery level adds 10% damage (sandbox option).
description=[*][b]Block:[/b] hold Martial Arts Core's Block key while aiming to raise a center guard with the sword, using Core's guard meter, perfect guard, and guard break.
description=[*][b]Deflecting gunfire:[/b] the block deflects bullets and soaks shotgun blasts, by your Form's mastery and Martial Arts level. Each deflection drains the guard meter; empty it and your guard breaks.
description=[*][b]Dodges:[/b] press the dodge key while aiming and you leap away with the hilt in your fist, the blade switched off for the leap and relit when you land.
description=[*]Between moves, the sword is held in Photon Sword's own stance.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options (Photon Sword Forms page)[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Damage per Form Mastery Level (%):[/b] default 10.
description=[*][b]Extinguish While Dodging:[/b] default on.
description=[*][b]Guard Drain from Gunfire:[/b] default 1.0; 0 means deflecting never drains the guard.
description=[/list]
description=Deflect chances and the rest are on Photon Sword's sandbox page.
description=
description=[h2]Mod options[/h2]
description=Photon Sword Dodge Animation (on; off plays Martial Arts Core's Pak Mei dodge) and Relight After Dodge (on).
description=
description=Requires Photon Sword, Senator's Martial Arms, and Senator Martial Arts Core. Build 42.20 or later. For Project A-Life or Bandits gunfire, add Photon Sword's patch mods.
